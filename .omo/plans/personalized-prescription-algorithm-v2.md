# personalized-prescription-algorithm-v2 - Work Plan

## TL;DR (For humans)
**What you'll get:** 800m·1500m·3000m·5000m 목표를 기록 유무와 분리해 선택하고, 근거가 부족해도 정직한 체감강도 계획을 받으며, 근거가 충분한 종목은 기존 상세 훈련과 새로 검수된 최대 2개 중 선수가 원하는 목적의 훈련을 직접 선택할 수 있습니다. 새 근거가 탈락한 종목은 현재 승인 훈련만 유지하고 공백을 공개합니다. 첫 두 후보는 `기본 보조훈련`과 `보조훈련 짧게`로 설명하며 고강도 처방과 훈련 횟수는 같고 보조 훈련 시간만 다릅니다. 승인한 다음 주기는 한 번만 안전하게 이어집니다.

**Why this approach:** 현재 고강도 상한을 더 올리지 않고 두 번째 후보에서 보조 훈련만 덜어내므로 마구잡이 증가를 피합니다. 정확한 훈련 숫자와 경기 배치는 공식 자료·논문을 다시 확인한 뒤 코칭 관점과 스포츠과학 관점이 같은 내용에 모두 동의할 때만 열립니다. 테이퍼는 별도 근거표에서 검토하되, 선수의 평소 훈련 기준과 정확한 수치 권한이 없으면 계획에 자동 적용하지 않습니다.

**What it will NOT do:** 일지·출석·포인트 때문에 훈련을 자동으로 늘리지 않습니다. 100~400m 스프린트, 종목 간 기록 환산, 검증되지 않은 테이퍼 비율이나 훈련량 공식은 열지 않습니다. 경기일을 넣었다는 이유로 숨은 감량·고강도 증가·오버로드를 만들지 않습니다. 메모 원문이나 월경 주기 추정도 처방 숫자에 사용하지 않습니다.

**Effort:** XL
**Risk:** High - 훈련 숫자, 저장 버전, 후보 무결성, 다음 주기 전환을 함께 바꾸므로 권한과 실패 시 원상복구가 핵심입니다.
**Decisions to sanity-check:** 고강도는 두 후보에서 동일하게 유지, 종목별 총 2~3개는 목표지만 근거 탈락 시 기존 1개 유지, 선수가 상세 훈련을 직접 선택, 이중 독립 검수 통과 시 대표님 추가 확인 없이 활성화, 경기일은 승인된 배치만 바꾸고 수치는 바꾸지 않음, 수치형 테이퍼 권한은 별도 오너 승인 전까지 미부여.

## Scope
### Must have
- Preserve the existing D9/hold gate, exact-event record anchoring, atomic detailed-prescription fallback, candidate hashing, SELF selection authority, and immutable active-frame behavior while extending the algorithm.
- Add an exact target event independent of the evidence record for 800 m, 1500 m, 3000 m, and 5000 m; sparse or cross-event evidence still produces an RPE-first plan.
- Make BALANCED and CONSERVATIVE observably different only through support duration. Both candidates keep identical session topology, QUALITY coordinates, intents, and detailed prescriptions.
- Keep BALANCED at the current product ceiling, without presenting that ceiling as a physiological optimum. CONSERVATIVE collapses applicable BASE/EASY support duration ranges to their minimum and never converts a session to REST in this wave.
- Target expansion of each supported event from one active detailed template to two or three total only when every exact entry passes source, transfer, population, authority, hash, expiry/revocation, and runtime tests. Zero additions is a valid evidence result; retain the existing approved entry and keep the event gap OPEN rather than filling a quota.
- Require the athlete to select an available detailed template by stated purpose. Do not automatically rank one template as optimal, safer, or superior without a separately approved selection rule.
- Apply the owner's delegated authority: an exact template or race-placement row may activate without another owner prompt only when independent coaching-applicability and sports-science/transfer lanes both approve the same artifact digest under the fixed gate. This delegation does not grant numeric taper authority.
- Create a taper evidence/authority matrix that records positive, null, adverse, youth, female/sex-transfer, and psychological evidence with exact baseline requirements. Keep every numeric row inactive in this implementation wave.
- Accept an optional target race date as a date-only transient input. It may select a source-reviewed placement preview that reorders already-approved sessions, but it cannot change total session content, prescription numbers, intensity, or support duration. Persistence and saved-plan activation remain disabled until a named privacy/governance receipt accepts purpose, retention event/duration, deletion/erase-all, export, youth age-out, and withdrawal behavior.
- Replace implicit sibling adaptation with a versioned transform registry. Main currently implements only the unversioned BALANCED-to-CONSERVATIVE reduction. Todo 1 must bind the owner's 2026-08-23 approval for the reverse increase as `APPROVED_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_ACTIVE`; Todo 7 may then activate exactly two duration-only VOLUME edges inside one immutable v3 pair: reduction compatibility `BALANCED_TO_CONSERVATIVE_EXISTING_SIBLING_ONLY` and approved restoration `CONSERVATIVE_TO_BALANCED_EXISTING_SIBLING_ONLY`. FREQUENCY and INTENSITY rows remain inactive pending separate owner authority. Every active successor edge is deterministic and provenance-bound.
- Add exactly-once activation of an accepted successor under the existing shared mutation lock with fresh D9/hold/integrity checks and rollback-complete failure behavior.
- Preserve the existing local-runtime 9.5-day/19-slot structural invariant and 7/9/10 projection model without claiming canonical promotion. Define completion against visible sessions through `ceil(projectionLengthDays)` and carry selected-plan/progress context into the next frame.
- Keep all implementation, specification, validators, storage, UI explanations, and browser flows synchronized.

### Must NOT have (guardrails, anti-slop, scope boundaries)
- No 100 m, 200 m, or 400 m sprint-plan activation; no ATP-PC detailed activation in this plan.
- No automatic increase from journals, completion, RPE, distance, mood, sleep, attendance, memo text, streaks, points, badges, or generic AI inference.
- No raw memo, symptom clause, evidence clause, or free text in plan inputs, hashes, authority records, audit, or adaptation decisions.
- No aggregate frame-load score, sRPE ceiling, percentage progression, operative/runtime taper percentage, cross-event pace conversion, or invented numeric prescription. Exact source-reported taper numbers remain preservable as inactive research observations.
- No claim that support-session options are athlete-load personalization, recovery optimization, or tapering; no `CONSERVATIVE` label implying global safety.
- No removal or mutation of a QUALITY session merely to make CONSERVATIVE look lighter.
- No use of division, sex, or age label alone as a dose multiplier. Youth remains eligible where the exact template's population gate passes; otherwise use RPE-only fallback rather than blocking plan access.
- No automatic rule from menstrual-cycle phase, inferred sex physiology, mood, sleep, or psychological state. Structured context may be displayed for athlete-controlled reflection or human review only.
- No direct runtime import from the research index or reconstructed catalog; catalog presence is not authority.
- No multi-race priority, heat/final round logic, or post-race transformation in this wave. Intake accepts at most one optional target race date and never chooses between competitions.
- No silent TEN_K or GENERAL_ENDURANCE support claim while the integrity parser remains limited to MIDDLE_DISTANCE and FIVE_K.
- No migration that rewrites existing v1/v2 bytes in place, and no production/deployment claim without an observed release receipt.
- No closure of an existing OPEN issue or canonical promotion unless a separate owner closure/promotion record and all issue-specific evidence exist. Source acquisition, a self-check, or this implementation alone is insufficient.
- No new backend, account synchronization, payment, remote-coach authorization, or medical-clearance semantics.
