# Wave 2 - Adversarial Plan Review

## Critical findings corrected

1. Session permutation is not automatically dose-neutral. It changes recovery spacing
   and can move work into or out of a 7/9/10-day projection. The plan now requires an
   exact projection, spacing authority, and no visibility-boundary crossing.
2. The two initial candidates do not use an accepted athlete-load baseline. They are
   support-work preferences, not baseline-personalized load or taper prescriptions.
3. PB/SB and explicit requests are triggers only. They do not supply the magnitude of
   a change; successor edges stay inside an already approved envelope.
4. Athlete access and evidence transfer are separate. Youth remains eligible, while
   youth and female/sex transfer are reviewed as distinct authority fields.
5. Template availability does not imply an optimal choice. The athlete explicitly
   selects a purpose; automatic ranking needs a separate accepted rule.
6. Product approval for an optional race date is not retention authority. Race input
   remains transient until a named privacy/governance receipt exists.
7. Local owner authority activates only the existing VOLUME sibling transition;
   FREQUENCY and INTENSITY transforms remain inactive.
