# Wave 1 - Direct Running Taper Evidence

## 800 m

- [Mujika et al., 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10694140/): eight trained
  men; neither tested six-day reduction significantly improved 800 m.
- [Mujika et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12165889/): nine men;
  daily training outperformed resting every third day in that protocol, but the arms
  contained only five and four runners.

## 1500 m

- [Shepley et al., 1992](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1559951/) used time to
  exhaustion at 1500 m pace, not an actual race.
- [Spilsbury et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30608885/) found a
  clearer response after a race-pace final session than after 115% race pace.
- [Spilsbury et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34062089/) found a
  greater response in a lower-volume/faster-final-session package, but changed both
  variables and included only eight men.

## 3000 m

- [Obata et al., 2023](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/rjsp/15/0/15_2243/_article/-char/en):
  seven junior-high boys; day-4 versus day-6 final stimulus showed no significant
  performance difference.
- [Obata and Hayashi, 2023/2024](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jspehssconf/73/0/73_162/_article/-char/ja):
  twelve junior-high boys; 21% versus 41% reduction showed no significant performance
  superiority.

## 5000 m

- [Houmard et al., 1994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8007812/): the positive
  run-taper arm contained eight athletes.
- [Houmard et al., 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2318562/): a three-week
  reduction maintained rather than improved 5 km race performance.
- [McConell et al., 1993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8440543/): reducing
  volume, frequency, and intensity preserved aerobic capacity but slowed nine of ten
  men over 5 km.

## Finding

The evidence supports taper as a researchable strategy and supports preserving
event specificity. It does not authorize one exact duration, reduction percentage,
frequency change, intensity change, or final-session offset for TrainOracle.
