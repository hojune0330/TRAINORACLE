# Intent Diff: Taper Deep Review

## Expected truths

- Taper is a distinct pre-competition policy, not a synonym for moving sessions around a race date.
- Any numeric taper needs an athlete-specific pre-taper training baseline in the same units as the reduction.
- Evidence must be separated by exact event, age, sex, training status, and outcome.
- A study protocol or group mean is not runtime authority for a universal percentage or final-session offset.
- Youth access is not categorically blocked, but adult/male values cannot be silently transferred.
- Mood, sleep, symptoms, or cycle phase may be shown as structured context; they do not independently authorize a dose increase or clearance.
- Multiple races or rounds require competition-anchor and bout semantics before taper logic can act on them.

## Observed reality before this review

- The existing plan had one race-placement todo and prohibited percentage tapering, but it did not give taper its own authority state, baseline gate, transfer matrix, or multi-race failure state.
- Local research already marks universal taper duration, reduction percentage, and last-MAIN offset as unknown/prohibited.
- Current owner direction separates a competition anchor from completed bouts, but the relevant decision packet remains non-runtime and leaves race priority, multi-race handling, taper template content, and post-race behavior unresolved.

## Required closure

- Expand the source gate with direct, null, indirect, and transfer-limited taper evidence.
- Split taper evidence acceptance from race-date placement in the implementation plan.
- Require truthful placement-only or review-required outcomes when numeric taper authority is absent.
