# Expansion Log

## Wave 1

- Internal authority lane: local Formation, competition-anchor, baseline, and youth-transfer documents.
- Direct taper lane: 800/1500/5000 and mixed endurance running studies.
- Transfer lane: youth, female athletes, menstrual-cycle evidence, and taper psychology.
- Adversarial lane: current plan failure modes.
- Orchestrator counter-search: null performance results, high-intensity variability, overreaching harms, and mood deterioration.

## Wave 2

- Direct under-18 search found two small male junior-high 3000 m studies. Neither established a superior timing or percentage rule.
- Event-specific search added 5 km maintenance and deterioration studies, a poorly reported youth 1500 m study, practice surveys, and direct 800/1500/3000/5000 counterevidence.
- Female/sex-transfer search found menstrual-cycle and taper-psychology reviews, but no controlled female running taper authority for 800-5000 m.
- Adversarial review found that session permutation can be a hidden taper and that PB/SB/request triggers do not create numeric authority.
- Local authority review confirmed that race priority, multi-race handling, numeric taper, and post-race behavior remain open.

## Convergence

No unchecked lead changes the current implementation boundary. The poorly indexed
2014 youth 1500 m paper is retained as low-confidence context only. Controlled female
running taper, adult flat-3000 m taper, and larger event-specific replications remain
future research gaps rather than reasons to invent defaults.

## Precision refresh - 2026-08-23

- Added Tonnessen et al. 2014 as descriptive elite endurance context with seven women;
  it does not transfer a skiing/biathlon peaking pattern into an exact running rule.
- Added O'Connor et al. 1989 as the opposite-direction female-swimming psychological
  observation to Taylor et al. 1997. The contrast blocks one-way mood claims and keeps
  self-report descriptive rather than dose-authoritative.
- A fresh search found no controlled female 800-5000 m running taper study and no
  controlled adult flat-3000 m taper trial that changed the existing `NOT_FOUND`
  cells. The numeric runtime authority decision therefore remains `NOT_GRANTED`.
