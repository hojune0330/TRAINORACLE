# Wave 2 - Transfer and Counterevidence

## Population transfer

- The direct junior-high 3000 m studies support youth access to reviewable plans, not
  a blanket youth block and not an exact youth default.
- No controlled female running taper trial for 800-5000 m was identified in the
  reviewed set. Male group means cannot be silently copied as female defaults.
- [McNulty et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32661839/) found trivial
  average menstrual-cycle performance effects with low-quality heterogeneous evidence.
  No cycle-phase dose rule is justified.

## Psychology and adverse response

- [Stone et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36696042/) found taper
  psychology research to be mostly adult, male, and non-running. Taper can reduce
  fatigue and can also be experienced as a stressor.
- [Taylor et al., 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9140908/) reported mood
  deterioration during reduced training in female swimmers, preventing mood from
  serving as a universal success surrogate.
- [Aubry et al., 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25134000/) found less
  favorable response and more infection after functional overreaching in male
  triathletes. A race date must not generate a pre-taper overload block.

## Product boundary

Structured context can support athlete reflection or human review. It cannot infer
readiness, clear D9, choose a percentage, increase intensity, or create a demographic
multiplier. Raw narrative remains outside plan generation.
