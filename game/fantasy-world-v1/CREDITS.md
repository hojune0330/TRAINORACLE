## New environment images

`ground.webp` and `panorama.webp` were generated with the **imagegen** tool on
**2026-10-17 (Asia/Seoul, KST)** for this project's original Starlight Relay
Forest setting. No seed was provided by the tool. The original generated PNGs
were converted to WebP with Pillow, quality 88, method 6, keeping their original
pixel dimensions. No crop, resizing, compositing or reference-image input was
used for these two assets. Private machine output paths are not published.
